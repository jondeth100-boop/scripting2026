The economy is bult around the fact that Fort McMurray is an oil town and is home to two major oil companies Syncrude and Suncor. The oilsands are a big part of the culture and people's lives as it exports oil to all over. For those that don't want to work for the oil sands or want to work adjacent to the rigs, there are always plenty of opportunities for people to work in the trades to keep the city running. Fort McMurray is home to a well-known trades college; Keyano college, it has lots of connections to the trades industry for job placement after you finish your schooling like carpentry, pluming, welding although the best placements are in the oil fields. The college also offers entry level courses in fine arts such as acting and art. The logging industry is also very prominent as lots of trees must be clearcut so new construction can begin due to the city's location in the boreal forest. Up in this region there is nowhere to expand except into the forest. Due to this, most people work in the city or on the edge of town as the oilsands are outside of town in Wood Buffalo County.
