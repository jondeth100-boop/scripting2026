The greatest attraction that to brings people to nature is the large natural lake. It allows for many water sports like swimming, wakeboarding and water skiing. With a large stock of fish there is enough fishing for everyone in both the summer and winter. Ice fishing is a grate activity for the hole family. Flanking the lake is the biking trails that wind and roll through the hills like a rollercoaster. You can take leisurely ride through some flat trails or whip around bends, up and down hills for the thrill of the ride and adrenalin. For those who want something a little slower there are plenty of beautiful hiking trails amongst the tall trees and nothing but the sound of birds for company. The trails will take you far from civilization for peaceful isolation. Then when the winter season hits all the trails that were used for biking and hiking are groomed for cross country skiing. The trails in the winter provide a varied collection of experiences. Also, the community maintains a groomed ice rink on the lake for hocky and ice skating. The frozen open lake is awesome for racing snow mobiles. The community youth host races that are a lot of fun, these races include back country trails.
