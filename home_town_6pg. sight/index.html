Gregoire Lake Estates is a small town out 30 minutes outside Fort McMurray, set deep in the Boreal forest out of the way from the rat race of life. The size of the town can't be understated as it's more of a village than a town. The population of Gregoire Lake Estates is 199 residents as of the last census. It is set back in the forest a little out of view from the main road with a barrier of trees separating the highway from the town. There are no shops or gas stations, it is a quiet little town where most come to get away form the city's hustle, crime and to embrace its family friendly environment where kids can go out without a care in the world. Although it's quiet, there are lots of things to do such as winding bike trails that whip through the trees like roller-coaster calm walking trails if you are looking for something a little slower. In the winter, you can race through the trees on snowmobiles or go for a leisurely cross country ski trip on nicely groomed trails and if that's not your speed there is a locally maintained ice rink.
