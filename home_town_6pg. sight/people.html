Among the people who inhabit this community it includes the gibbs family. They stay to themselves these days whereas they use to be a maigre part of the community. On the other hand, the neighbours are friendly and inclusive. They will come when you need help, such as if a tree falls on the roof of your house. You will have more hands than you'll need, that also includes helping people move in. For the most part the neighbours are quiet witch makes the place peaceful and serene perfect for family rearing and retirement. For those who are interested and want to get to know the neighbours or be active in the community, there are plenty of private parties and gatherings on the weekends for you to get to know everyone. Other than that, they mostly keep to themselves and to their own lives. This isn't to say that they are rude, everyone is just quiet. Especially during the day when a lot of the population goes to work wile the kids go to school in the city fort McMurry. The population of Gregoire Lake Estates is 199 residents according to the municipal census conducted by the Regional Municipality of Wood Buffalo.
